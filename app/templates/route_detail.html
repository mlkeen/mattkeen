INTERPRETATION

About the measures.

Ridership uses FRA Total Ridership (Mktg) values, excluding system aggregates from individual route listings. Customer OTP uses the route-level C OTP proportion and is displayed only when FRA's complete service and subservice labels agree with the named ridership route. Year-over-year ridership changes are percentages; OTP differences are percentage points. Original workbooks and Excel row numbers remain available for review.

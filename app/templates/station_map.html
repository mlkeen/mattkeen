INTERPRETATION

Passengers, not trains.

Each station's lateness percentage is late detraining passengers ÷ all detraining passengers. Counts are summed before calculating percentages across trains and quarters.

Stations are matched by the three-letter station code against USDOT/BTS locations. Unmatched or ambiguous coordinates are excluded and counted in the publisher's QA report. The map is not a train on-time-performance metric and does not imply delay causation.

Sources: FRA quarterly Station Performance Metric tables; USDOT/BTS NTAD Amtrak Stations (2026 snapshot). Station location dataset.

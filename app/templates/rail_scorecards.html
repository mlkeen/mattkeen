METRIC DEFINITIONS

Revenue yield is not a fare.

Passenger revenue per rider = FRA-reported passenger revenue ÷ quarterly total ridership (marketing). Passenger revenue per passenger-mile = the same passenger revenue ÷ reported passenger-miles. Both are nominal-dollar yield measures, not necessarily the price paid by an individual passenger.

Cost recovery = adjusted operating revenue ÷ allocated operating expense (as reported). Customer OTP uses only exact, unambiguous whole-service matches, not averages of train or subservice rates.

Revenue is taken from the FRA's financial passenger-revenue field, not its operating-revenue field. Financials are joined to ridership by identical fiscal quarter, APT code, and reported route name. Negative passenger revenue, inconsistent revenue entries, and nonpositive ridership/passenger-mile denominators do not produce yield ratios. Route-level financial rows never include system aggregates.

Financial source values and reporting units can contain accounting adjustments. YoY growth compares the same fiscal quarter one year earlier. These rankings describe operations; they do not measure economic welfare or benefit–cost performance.

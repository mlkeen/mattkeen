INTERPRETATION

Cost recovery isn't profitability.

Cost recovery divides operating revenue by allocated operating expense. The avoidable and fully allocated expense measures use passenger revenue and have separate treatments of state operating payments. These series should not be conflated.

These ratios are not benefit–cost ratios, economic profits, or measures of social value. Financial accounting adjustments can generate zero or negative expense denominators; those observations are retained with source references but excluded from the chart rather than silently transformed.

Source: FRA quarterly Financial Metrics. The import distinguishes route-level rows from Amtrak system aggregates and preserves original reporting labels.

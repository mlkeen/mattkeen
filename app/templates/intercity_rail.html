METHOD

Traceable data, not placeholder statistics.

01Acquire

Preserve the original FRA quarterly workbooks.

02Extract

Standardize fiscal periods and service/subservice identifiers with source provenance.

03Validate

Check fractions, unique keys, missing periods, and accounting totals. Do not average percentage rates without weights.

04Publish

Publish ridership and route-level OTP as separate auditable measures.

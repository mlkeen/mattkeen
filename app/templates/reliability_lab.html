READING THE RELATIONSHIP

Association is not causation.

Horizontal: customer OTP change from the same quarter a year earlier, in percentage points. Vertical: ridership change from the same quarter a year earlier, in percent. A positive slope means that higher OTP changes tend to coincide with greater ridership growth in the selected observations.

The optional line is pooled ordinary least squares and is descriptive only. Route–quarter observations from the same service are not statistically independent; seasonality, service changes, fares, capacity, and other factors can confound the relationship. The tool does not estimate causal effects or claim statistical significance.

Only exact, unambiguous whole-service OTP matches are included. Subservice OTP figures are never averaged or attached to a route without a verified crosswalk. Missing values and negative ridership adjustments are excluded, never treated as zero.

Source: FRA quarterly financial and customer OTP workbooks, FY2021 Q4 onward. Earliest usable YoY pairs require a corresponding quarter in the previous fiscal year.

This is a screening scenario. 2022 NHTS survey-weighted trip frequency can anchor the national market size, while FY2025 Amtrak totals calibrate a separate corridor-wide scale; neither supplies county-pair OD counts.

Calibration disclosure: With the nationwide county catalog and local FY2025 calibration published, the total demand level is fitted to actual Amtrak route ridership. The 2022 NHTS provides a national person-weighted long-distance trip-rate benchmark only after its source file is published locally. OD splits, travel-choice parameters, station geography, and service assumptions are not calibrated. No passenger-level ticket OD or NHTS county-pair flows have been used. Otherwise the model remains entirely illustrative.

    Method and limitations

    County population measures market size, county-centroid distances proxy for alignment, and a gravity-style distance decay estimates total potential trips. An assumed logit relates mode choice to generalized travel costs. With calibration published, one shared multiplicative factor is fitted using complete FY2025 route totals from normalized FRA Amtrak performance tables. When published locally, 2022 NHTS V2.1 long-distance person microdata provide a weighted annualized 50+ mile trip frequency (`LONGDIST`, `WTPERFIN`), and descriptive annual Amtrak usage (`LD_AMT`). Re-run Amtrak calibration whenever this profile changes. The NHTS most-recent-trip mode is not a rail mode-share elasticity. No coefficients for mode choice, frequency, station access or distance decay have been estimated from behavioral microdata.

    For defensible investment analysis, the next stages are station-level on/off data or ticket OD matrices, observed route frequencies/fare and speed, NHTS and other trip-distance distributions, actual rail-network geography, access catchments, and explicit competition/transfer modeling. A corridor-wide adjustment is not a substitute for these. The 2022 NHTS household survey does not furnish a county-level OD demand matrix; FHWA's passive OD products use larger analysis zones.

    Source references: ACS 2024 5-year · Census county Gazetteer 2025 · Amtrak reference reports · 2022 NHTS V2.1 downloaded microdata · 2022 NHTS Codebook. The seed demo county numbers are fictional demonstration inputs until you run the Census publisher.

INTERPRETATION

Minutes are not a rate.

This dashboard sums FRA delay minutes by train, host railroad, responsibility and cause code. The underlying train-mile column repeats for each cause code and is deliberately excluded from these totals.

Do not interpret total delay minutes as the number of late passengers, the share of trains arriving late, or delay per 10,000 train-miles. These answer different questions. Subservices remain separate to avoid accidentally mixing reporting units.

Source: FRA quarterly Delay Metrics, DelayandMileage sheets.

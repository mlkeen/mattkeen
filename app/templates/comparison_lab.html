READING THE DATA

How to compare.

Absolute ridership shows scale. Indexed ridership shows proportional change from a shared baseline. Year-over-year growth compares each quarter with the same quarter a year earlier, not the previous quarter.

Customer OTP is a fraction of passengers arriving on time, not a percentage of trains. The chart preserves reported subservice boundaries and does not average percentages without weights. Observations missing from the FRA record appear as gaps rather than zeros.

Source: FRA quarterly financial metrics and route-level customer on-time performance workbooks. Service names are kept as reported in their respective workbooks.

Source: user-provided `ridership.csv` generated by `scripts/fra_etl.py` from FRA quarterly financial workbooks.

- 1,076 total records, including 1,021 named-service route records and 55 aggregate records.
- 20 consecutive fiscal quarters, FY2021 Q4–FY2026 Q3.

- Every fiscal quarter's route-level ridership sum reconciles to the `APT_All_APT` system total (0.02-rider tolerance). Never add the other system total (`APT_RT_NTS`) or the adjustment rows on top of route data.

The QA checks validate extraction consistency, key uniqueness and aggregate reconciliation. They do **not** constitute independent verification against FRA originals or a broader assessment of each underlying route-level statistical definition.
